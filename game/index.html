PALISADE

A lantern duel of pips, tints and nerve — you against Warden Ash.

  1. Place one card a round into Hearth (×1), Market (×2) or Spire (×3). Five rounds, six cards — one stays in your hand.
  2. Higher pip takes the lane. Match the tint below your card to anchor it — an anchored card needs 2 more pips to beat.
  3. Ember ★ is wild: it anchors with any tint. Your one Shift retints a placed card.
  4. At the end, each lane pays stake × top pip in lanterns 🏮. Most lanterns wins.

Tints are colour and shape: Rust ▲ orange, Ash ■ blue, Verd ● green, Ember ★ gold.
Click a card, then a lane. Keys 1–6 pick a card, Esc cancels.
